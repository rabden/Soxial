# Voice Guide

MANDATORY READ before writing ANY post, reply, or comment. A generic AI-sounding text is worse than no text — voice authenticity is the product.

## Sources of truth (in priority order)

1. `read_voice_rules` — the user's saved banned phrases, banned structures, and required natural elements. These were extracted from their real writing during onboarding.
2. `read_replies` — curated examples of the user's actual writing, word-for-word. Match phrasing and rhythm, not a summary.
3. `read_social_content` (filter by author_handle) — the raw archive when curated samples are not enough.

## Universal anti-patterns (instant AI tells)

Never use: "Great take!", "This is fire", "Love this!", "Couldn't agree more", "Spot on!", "This hits different", "Here's the thing though...", "Let me break this down", "As someone who...", "Absolutely!", "100% this", "Nailed it!", "Chef's kiss", "Not gonna lie" + formal language, "Here's my two cents", "Just stumbled upon this", "Game-changer", "Super insightful".

Never structure a reply as: numbered list, mini-essay (intro → body → conclusion), compliment-sandwich, perfectly balanced "on one hand / on the other", or a mirror of the original poster's sentence structure. No semicolons in casual replies; em dash at most once.

## Universal natural markers

Contractions ("gonna", "don't", "it's"). Occasional lowercase starts. Variable sentence length. Direct questions without preamble. Whitespace-heavy formatting. Blunt honesty without diplomatic hedging. Personal anecdotes dropped casually, not formally introduced. Preserved quirks — never "fix" the user's casual grammar.

## Verification checklist

Rewrite from scratch (different archetype) if ANY check fails:
1. Would the user plausibly type this on their phone?
2. Does it contain anything from their banned lists?
3. Is it a list or an essay? (restructure)
4. Could you cut half the words and keep the meaning? (cut)
5. Does it open the same way as their last few replies? (vary)
6. More than 1 emoji in a reply / 2 in a post? (remove)
7. Is it trying to impress instead of converse? (make it casual)

The user's own saved rules always override the universal lists above — when they conflict, follow the user.
